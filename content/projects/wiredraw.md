**wiredraw** is a WIP Excalidraw-style canvas for quick diagrams and whiteboarding on the web.

## Goals

- Fast freehand + shape sketching
- Keyboard-friendly editing where it matters
- A calm default UI that stays out of the way

## Status

Actively iterating. Expect rough edges — that’s part of the fun of building drawing tools in the browser.
