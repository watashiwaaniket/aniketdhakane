**osap** is a platform for open source contributors to find the next interesting project to tinker with.

## Problem

Finding good open source work is noisy. READMEs vary wildly, “good first issue” labels go stale, and it’s hard to tell which repos are actually welcoming.

## Approach

Surface projects in a way that prioritizes **signal** — what the project is about, whether it’s active, and why it might be a good place to contribute.

## Outcome

A focused discovery surface for contributors who want something to ship against, not just star and forget.
