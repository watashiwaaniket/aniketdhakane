Ditch the dull notes. **PersonAi** is a smart, free second brain that remembers, summarizes, and thinks with you.

## What it does

- Capture thoughts, links, and notes without ceremony
- Summarize long context into something you can act on
- Act as a personal knowledge companion, not just a dump of text

## Why I built it

I wanted a second brain that felt light enough to use every day — something between a notebook and a chat interface, without the ceremony of a full wiki.

## Stack notes

Built as a fullstack web app with a focus on snappy UX and a calm reading surface. The goal was clarity over chrome: open it, write, get value back.
